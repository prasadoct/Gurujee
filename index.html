About Guruji · परिचय

The family priest Pune households call first

For over twenty-five years, Ramakant Guruji has been the priest many Pune families call first — for a new home, a newborn’s naming, a Ganpati at home, or a parent’s annual shraddha.

He learned the rituals at his father’s side, then through formal study at a Ved Pathshala in Pune, and performs every ceremony with full traditional accuracy in the Deshastha, Marathi Bhatji tradition. What families remember most, though, is how he explains: before each step he tells you, in plain Marathi, Hindi or English, what the mantra means and why it is done. Young couples doing their very first puja and elders who know every verse both leave feeling at ease.

      Good to know

      Questions families often ask

      Anything else? Message Guruji on WhatsApp.

      What does “samagri included” mean?

      Guruji brings all the puja materials — haldi-kunku, supari, sacred threads, havan samagri and so on. Your family only arranges fresh items like fruit, flowers, milk and prasad. Each ceremony lists exactly what’s on your side, and you’ll get the list on WhatsApp after booking.

      When and how do we pay the dakshina?

      After the ceremony, by UPI or cash. There is no advance for bookings in Pune and PCMC. The final amount within the range depends on the scale of the ceremony and is confirmed on the phone call — before the day, not on it.

      We don’t know our gotra or the right muhurat. Is that a problem?

      Not at all — this is very common. Guruji will help find the muhurat for your date, and uses a traditional alternative sankalp when the gotra isn’t known.

      Can relatives join by video call?

      Yes. Many families have grandparents or siblings watching from elsewhere. Guruji will make sure they can hear the explanations too.

      What if we need to change the date?

      Call or WhatsApp at least 48 hours before, and Guruji will move it to the next date that suits you. There’s no charge to reschedule.
